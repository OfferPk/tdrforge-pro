# CCSDS 652.0-M-2 & ISO 16363 Trustworthy Digital Repository (TDR) Specification

**Document**: CCSDS 652.0-M-2 (Magenta Book, December 2024 / ISO 16363:2012)  
**Accreditation Standard**: ISO 16919:2014 (CCSDS 652.1-M-3)  
**Foundational Model**: ISO 14721:2012 (OAIS / CCSDS 650.0-M-3)  
**System**: TDRForge Pro (v1.0.0)

## 1\. Executive Summary & Scope

CCSDS 652.0-M-2 (ratified internationally as ISO 16363:2012) specifies the formal criteria, audit methodology, and evidence validation framework for assessing and certifying long-term digital preservation repositories as Trustworthy Digital Repositories (TDR).

## 2\. Core Audit Sections & Evaluation Metrics

- Section 3: Organizational Infrastructure (§3): Governance, strategic planning, funding models, and staffing.  
- Section 4: Digital Object Management (§4): SIP ingest agreements (PAIS), AIP creation, PDI provenance, and automated SHA-256 fixity audits.  
- Section 5: Infrastructure & Security Risk Management (§5): System resilience, physical security, and multi-site disaster recovery.
